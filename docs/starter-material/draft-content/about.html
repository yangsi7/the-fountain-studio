My Journey to This Work

Like many who find their way to healing work, my path wasn't linear. My background in Transpersonal Psychology gave me a deep understanding of the human psyche and the connection between our inner world and outer experience.

But it was my own journey of healing that led me to understand a fundamental truth: nobody else could give me what I needed except for me. This realization shifted everything—from seeking external fixes to trusting my own inner intelligence.

I discovered that our bodies are electrical systems, constantly communicating through frequencies and patterns. When I found Biofield Tuning and the Gyrotonic® Method, I experienced firsthand how gentle, non-invasive approaches could create profound shifts without forcing or pushing.

Why I Don't Want to "Fix" You

I'm not trying to make a big business out of this work, and I'm not trying to be famous. This is intimate, individual work where you're seen as the unique system you are—not one of my gazillions.

I've witnessed too much spiritual commercialism that sells promises of grandiosity. Healing is messy, often slow unwinding. The promise of "activations" and "enlightenment" misses the point entirely.

My Approach: The 'Bio-Electrician'

I think of myself as a 'bio-electrician' using sound and frequency to work with your electrical system:

...to add more voltage to your electrical system

You feel lighter, heal faster

...to clear the static in your system that blocks out the clarity of your truth

You feel true, make positive changes

...to calm and support the regulation of the nervous system

You feel more relaxed and you can Respond instead of React

This isn't about raising your vibration or leaving the physical body. All frequencies need to be online, all vibrations online. We honor the sadness, the heaviness, the lightness. Our feet are on this earth and this earth needs us.
