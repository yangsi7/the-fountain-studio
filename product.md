## Product Overview

### Name

### Tagline
Building coherence in a chaotic world, one body, one field at a time.

### Vision
A world where people can easily access holistic healing practices that help them reconnect with their natural flow, release accumulated stress and trauma, and rediscover who they truly are beneath the noise of modern life. Where wellness is not about becoming someone else, but about clearing the static to return to your authentic self.

### Problem Statement
**What pain exists in the world?**

Modern life creates constant electrical and emotional "static" in our bodies and energy fields. People experience chronic stress, disconnection from their bodies, unresolved emotional patterns, poor posture from sedentary work, shallow breathing from anxiety, and a pervasive sense of being "out of alignment."

Traditional healthcare focuses on symptoms rather than addressing the body's electrical and energetic systems. Meanwhile, the wellness industry is fragmented and overwhelming - people don't know what modalities to choose, how to book sessions, or whether practitioners are qualified. Many suffer in silence, using ineffective workarounds (medication, passive treatments, disconnected fitness routines) that never address the root cause: their nervous system is dysregulated and their biofield is incoherent.

### Market Context
- **TAM (Total Addressable Market)**: Health-conscious adults in Switzerland (particularly Zürich area) seeking complementary healing practices. Includes professionals experiencing burnout, individuals dealing with chronic stress/pain, people recovering from trauma, and those seeking embodied wellness beyond conventional medicine. Demographic skews toward women 30-55 with higher education and disposable income for wellness investments.

- **Current Solutions**:
  - Traditional physiotherapy (treats symptoms, not energy field)
  - Standard yoga/Pilates studios (focus on physical only, lack specialized equipment and energy work)
  - Generic massage therapy (passive, temporary relief)
  - Wellness spas (luxury-focused, not therapeutic)
  - Online wellness courses (no personalized guidance, no hands-on treatment)
  - Fragmented practitioners (requires finding separate specialists for movement, breathwork, energy work)

- **Opportunity**:
  - **Integration Gap**: No one combines biofield tuning (energy work) + Gyrotonic® (specialized movement) + structured breathwork in one boutique setting
  - **Practitioner Credibility**: Qualified, certified practitioner with transpersonal psychology background (rare combination)
  - **Swiss Precision**: Swiss market values quality, precision, professionalism - currently underserved by generic wellness offerings
  - **Digital Friction**: Existing practitioners have clunky booking processes, unclear service descriptions, poor multilingual support
  - **Why Now**: Post-pandemic heightened awareness of nervous system health, growing acceptance of complementary medicine, increasing burnout rates among professionals

## Product Principles (Philosophy)

**Our Core Beliefs About How This Product Should Work**

1. **Return, Don't Transform**
   - **Belief**: Healing is not about becoming someone else or "fixing" what's broken. It's about clearing the static (distortion, trauma, dysregulation) so people can return to who they truly are beneath the noise.
   - **How this shows up**: All messaging focuses on "reconnect," "return," "rediscover" (not "transform," "improve," "optimize"). Sessions are framed as "clearing" and "integration" (not "fixing problems"). Success is when clients say "I feel like myself again."

2. **Body as Electrical System**
   - **Belief**: The body operates as a bio-electrical system with its own electromagnetic field. Coherent frequencies help the system reorganize itself. Working at this level creates lasting change (vs. temporary symptom management).
   - **How this shows up**: "Bio-electrician" positioning. Clear explanation of biofield science (not vague "energy"). Tuning forks and frequencies as tools (concrete, not mystical). Integration of movement (physical body) with biofield work (energetic body) with breathwork (nervous system regulation).

3. **Gentle Precision, Not Force**
   - **Belief**: Healing happens through gentle, precise interventions that allow the body to reorganize itself - not through force, pushing, or overriding the body's wisdom. Swiss precision applied to wellness.
   - **How this shows up**: "Gentle" and "flowing" language throughout. Specialized equipment (Gyrotonic®) for precise movements. Practitioner listens and attunes (not prescriptive protocols). Clients are never pushed beyond what feels safe. Premium aesthetic signals precision and quality (Swiss expectations).

4. **Integration Over Fragmentation**
   - **Belief**: Humans are whole systems - mind, body, emotions, energy field cannot be separated. Addressing one level while ignoring others leads to incomplete healing. True transformation requires integration across all dimensions.
   - **How this shows up**: Combination of three modalities (biofield, movement, breath) addresses multiple levels simultaneously. 90-minute Integration Sessions offer complete system reset. Kristen's transpersonal psychology background enables work with emotional/psychological alongside physical/energetic.

**Trade-offs We Accept**:
- **Boutique over scale**: Small, one-practitioner studio means limited availability and higher prices (vs. generic wellness studio with multiple practitioners). We accept this to maintain quality, deep attunement, and specialized expertise.
- **Education-required positioning**: "Bio-electrician" and "biofield tuning" require explanation (vs. familiar terms like "massage" or "therapy"). We accept this because precise language attracts right clients who want to understand what they're doing.
- **Local-first**: Focus on in-person sessions at Au ZH studio (vs. online offerings or multiple locations). We accept this because hands-on work with biofield and specialized equipment can't be replicated virtually.

**What We Are NOT**:
- **Not a spa**: No luxury indulgence framing, no passive pampering. This is therapeutic healing work (sometimes emotional, sometimes intense).
- **Not generic wellness**: Not offering services everyone else offers (standard massage, basic yoga, generic therapy). Specialized modalities requiring specific training.
- **Not "fast fitness"**: Not promising quick fixes or aggressive transformations. Sustainable, gentle, integrated approach that respects the body's timeline.
- **Not mystical or hippie**: Grounded in science (biofield physics, nervous system regulation, anatomy). Premium aesthetic, Swiss professionalism.
